Gen 2.
Forged for the field.

Everything Gen 1 taught us, rebuilt to live outside on a real hive: more sensors, solar power, Wi-Fi and a sealed case.

01Sensors

Six ways it reads the hive.

Brood temperature

Probes between the frames. Bees keep brood near 35 °C, so a drop is an early warning.

DS18B20 waterproof probes

CO₂

Rises and falls with colony size and activity.

Sensirion SCD-41

Inside humidity

Too damp breeds mold and disease. Too dry stresses brood.

Sensirion SHT45

Outside weather

Temperature, humidity and pressure outside, so inside readings have context.

BME280

Hive weight

Honey coming in, stores running low, and the sudden drop of a swarm.

100 kg load cell + HX711

Sound

The colony's hum can change with queen problems or swarming.

Piezo sensor + MCP6002 amplifier

02Built for outside

Power, connection, protection.

Solar powered

A 5.5 W panel and a solar charger keep a 10,000 mAh battery topped up through cloudy stretches.

Voltaic 6 V panel · CN3791 MPPT charger · fused

Wi-Fi to the cloud

Readings go to the CombGuard dashboard every 15 minutes, day and night.

ESP32 Feather V2

Sealed case

UV-resistant ASA print, gasket seal and a breathable vent that keeps water out.

ASA · PTFE vent · PG7 glands · GX12 connectors

What changed.

SensorsSound and basic readingsSix: brood temp, CO₂, humidity, weather, weight, sound
Enclosure3D-printed caseSealed ASA case with gasket, vent and weatherproof connectors
AlertsNoneGreen, yellow, red status and email alerts
