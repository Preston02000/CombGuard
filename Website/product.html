CombGuard Gen 2

A guard for every hive.

Five parts that work together to keep watch on one colony, day and night, without lifting the lid.

Join the WatchBeekeeper survey ↗

01 / 04

The case

One case on the side of the hive.

It holds the brains and the battery, and the gold hexagon light shows it's on watch.

Inside

Our own circuit boards.

We designed the main board ourselves. An ESP32 runs it, with a real-time clock so every reading is stamped, and a microSD card that keeps a backup copy.

The plugs

Sealed against the weather.

Every sensor plugs into a screw-lock GX12 connector. Rain stays out, and any part can be swapped in the yard.

The power

Charged by the sun.

A small panel on top of the case tops up the battery each day. No cords, no outlet in the yard.

The parts

Take a closer look.

Tap a part to see what it does and where it goes.

The case. Mounts on the side of the hive and holds the main board and battery.

  • Our own Gen 2 main board
  • ESP32 Feather inside
  • Clock and microSD backup

Sensor pod. Sits on the inner cover over the hole, sensors facing down into the hive behind a breathable vent membrane, so the bees can't coat them in propolis.

  • CO₂: Sensirion SCD41
  • Temperature and humidity: SHT45
  • Microphone: Knowles MEMS mic

Brood probe. A thin steel probe that slides down between two brood frames.

  • Tracks brood nest temperature
  • Healthy brood sits near 94°F

Scale. Two plates with a load cell between them. The whole hive sits on top.

  • Load cell rated to 220 lb (100 kg)
  • HX711 weight reader

Solar panel. Mounts on top of the case and keeps the battery charged.

  • Tilting mount
  • No cords to the yard

Outside sensor. A temperature and humidity sensor tucked inside the case, right behind the side vent, so it reads the open air and you can compare inside and out.

  • Behind the vent on the side of the case
  • Nothing extra to mount

    Tech specs

    The details.

    Measures
    Hive weight, brood temperature, CO₂, humidity, sound, outside temperature and humidity
    Sensors
    Sensirion SCD41 (CO₂), SHT45 (temperature and humidity), Knowles MEMS microphone, load cell with HX711, brood temperature probe, outside temperature and humidity sensor behind the case vent
    Brains
    ESP32 Feather on our own Gen 2 main board, real-time clock, microSD backup
    Connectivity
    2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Long-range LoRa planned for Gen 3
    Power
    Small solar panel and rechargeable battery
    Connectors
    Screw-lock GX12, sealed against rain
    Scale
    Load cell rated to 220 lb (100 kg)
    Fits
    Standard Langstroth hives
