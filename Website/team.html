The team

A student team, building from the board up.

We're a small team of students designing CombGuard ourselves, from the circuit boards and code to the case and this website.

The work behind it

Five kinds of work, one guard.

Hardware

Designing the main board and sensor pod, choosing every sensor and wiring it all together.

Firmware

The code on the ESP32 that takes readings, stamps the time, keeps a backup and sends them home.

Software

The live hive dashboard and the alerts that tell you which hive to open.

Design

The case, the brand and making a sensor kit feel at home on a beehive.

Research

Talking with beekeepers about what they check, what they miss and what they'd trust.

Meet the team
