AboutCombGuard

Built to
keep watch.

CombGuard is a student engineering project with one goal: help beekeepers catch trouble inside the hive before it's too late.

01Why we exist

Problems grow where no one can see them.

Beekeepers can only check a hive by opening it, and they can't do that every day. In the gaps between inspections, mites spread, brood chills and swarms prepare unseen. We're building a way to see into those gaps.

02Our name

Every colony posts guards at its gate.

Guard bees stand at the hive entrance and check everything that comes in. CombGuard is meant to be one more guard, for the comb inside. We started as HiveMind and renamed the project as its purpose got clearer.

03What we believe

Three rules we build by.

Don't disturb the colony.

Every inspection stresses a hive. CombGuard should mean fewer of them, not more.

Built for real apiaries.

Hives sit in fields, not next to outlets. So CombGuard runs on solar power and survives the weather.

Data you can act on.

Not a wall of numbers. A clear green, yellow or red, and an alert when something needs you.
